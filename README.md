### 🚀 About Me

I am a **Mechatronics & Embedded Systems Engineer** (Ain Shams University, Class of 2024) specializing in bare-metal C/C++ firmware, custom multi-layer PCB design, connected IoT telemetry pipelines, and rapid engineering automation.

- 🔬 **Hardware & Firmware:** Deep experience designing and bringing up mission-critical embedded systems across Egypt and KSA (ARM Cortex-M, ESP32, FreeRTOS, BLE/GATT, RF, CAN, Modbus).
- ⚡ **Rapid Engineering Automation:** Python pipelines for web scraping, headless bot workflows, data synthesis, and Model Context Protocol (MCP) agents.
- 🏛️ **BIM & Parametric 3D:** Autodesk Revit parametric modeling & Dynamo scripting, Blender 3D procedural workflows, and SolidWorks CAD.
- 🎨 **Creative Media:** Precision visual compositing and lighting in Photoshop, After Effects motion graphics, and Premiere post-production.
